**About Me:**
Hi! I'm Soham Pradhan, and I am a high school student interested in applying machine learning to complex biological systems, particularly in areas like computational pathology and cancer biology. **Project/Research Interests:**
I am primarily interested in using machine learning to analyze biomedical imaging and molecular data, especially in the context of diseases like cancer or neurological diseases.
