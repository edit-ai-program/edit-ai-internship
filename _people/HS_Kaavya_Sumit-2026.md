**Project/Research Interests:**
Given my interest in potentially pursuing neuroscience as a college major, I am especially interested in working on a project related to computational neuroscience. Math is one of my strongest subjects in school, and I enjoy applying quantitative thinking to practical, real-world problems. Research in computational neuroscience would allow me to explore the brain through mathematical models, statistical methods, and computational tools.

One possible area I am interested in is analyzing structural brain data, such as hippocampal volume or cortical thickness, in relation to cognitive or behavioral markers, such as sleep patterns, that may be passively collected through a smartphone app. I think this type of project would be exciting because it connects neuroscience, data analysis, and everyday behavior.

I am also interested in artificial intelligence and how it can be leveraged to advance biomedical research, including improving how researchers analyze complex biological and neurological data.
